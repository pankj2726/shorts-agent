# Banned Topics

The agent must refuse to script or publish a Short on any of these topics.
QA Gate #1 checks against this list automatically.

## Absolute bans (no exceptions)
- Medical diagnosis, treatment, or dosing advice stated as certain
- Legal advice stated as certain (what you *must* do legally)
- Investment/financial advice stated as certain ("buy X", "X will go up")
- Content that could be construed as medical, legal or financial advice without a clear disclaimer
- Real person's likeness or cloned voice without written consent
- Copyrighted scripts, footage, or music
- Hate speech based on identity
- Misinformation or deliberate falsehoods
- Sexual or violent content
- Content targeting or harmful to minors
- Spam or artificially viral content (fake lists, repetitive hooks with swapped words)

## Context-dependent bans (flag for human review)
- Health tips that could replace professional advice
- Political or religious controversy
- Crypto, NFT, or high-risk investment promotion
- Affiliate or sponsored content (needs #ad disclosure — flag, do not auto-publish)

- (e.g., competitor channel names — never mention by name negatively)
- (e.g., topics outside the niche that dilute the channel)
