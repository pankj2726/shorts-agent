# Hook Rules

The first 3 seconds determine whether the viewer swipes away. These rules are non-negotiable.

## Mandatory
1. **First frame** already shows motion or a bold caption. No logo, no channel intro, no black frame.
2. **Voice starts at 0.0 s.** Zero silence at the start.
3. **Hook ≤ 12 words.** One sentence. Count them.
4. **Captions appear within 0.3 s** and highlight the current word in a contrasting colour.
5. **Visual changes every 2–3 seconds** throughout the Short.

## Hook patterns that work (pick one per Short)
- **Mistake reveal:** "Most people get [X] completely wrong."
- **Specific number:** "This one habit saves 2 hours every day."
- **Before/after:** "I went from [bad state] to [good state] in [timeframe]."
- **Cost frame:** "The reason [X] costs you [Y] every month."
- **Action imperative:** "Do this before you [common action]."
- **Surprising fact:** "[Counterintuitive claim] — and here's the proof."
- **Question bait:** "Why do [most people] [do counterproductive thing]?"

## Patterns to avoid
- Greeting ("Hey guys", "Welcome back", "Hi everyone")
- Vague tease ("You won't believe what I found")
- Channel intro ("On this channel we...")
- Empty filler ("In today's video...")
- Rhetorical yes ("Do you want to earn more money?")

## A/B testing rule
Run two hook styles per week on similar topics. After 48 hours, compare 3-second hold rate in YouTube Analytics. Keep the winner's style in `learnings.md`.

## Hook quality checklist (QA Gate #1)
- [ ] ≤ 12 words
- [ ] No greeting or intro
- [ ] Opens a curiosity gap, makes a bold backed claim, or states a specific number
- [ ] Can be backed up by the video body
- [ ] Different from the last 5 hooks used (check DB)
